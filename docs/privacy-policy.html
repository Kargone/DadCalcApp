DadCalc Privacy Policy

Effective October 17, 2026

In short: DadCalc does not collect, share or sell any personal information. Everything you type stays on your device.

Who we are

DadCalc is a measurement calculator for Android made by Karson, an independent developer. This policy explains how the app handles your information.

Information we collect

None. DadCalc has no accounts, no analytics, no advertising and no crash reporting. The app does not request internet access, so it has no way to send anything off your device.

Information stored on your device

So it can pick up where you left off, DadCalc saves the following on your device only:

  • The calculation currently on the input screen
  • Your last 10 saved calculations (history)
  • Your settings, such as the output unit, fraction display and rounding

You can clear your history from the History screen. Uninstalling the app, or clearing its storage in your device's settings, deletes all of this information.

Device backups

If you have turned on Android backup, Android may include this saved information in your device backup so it can be restored to a new phone. That backup is handled by Google under your Google account and its privacy policy. The developer of DadCalc cannot see or access it.

Sharing

DadCalc does not share any information with anyone, because it does not collect any.

Children

DadCalc does not collect personal information from anyone, including children under 13.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
